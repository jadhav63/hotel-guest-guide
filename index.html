TV & Remote Issues

  • TV Input: Ensure TV is on HDMI 1.
  • Power: Check if the power cable behind the TV is plugged in tightly.
  • Not Working? Unplug the black DirecTV box behind the TV for 10 seconds, then plug back in.
  • Remote Tip: The box is mounted behind the TV. Point the remote specifically at the side/back of the TV for best signal.

Microwave & Clock

  • Microwave Door: Has a Child Safety Lock. Press the Unlock button first, then open the door.
  • Alarm Clock USB: To charge via USB, ensure the side knob is set to FM.

Keys & Ice Machines

  • Room Keys: Keep away from mobile phones and credit cards to prevent deactivation.
  • Ice Machines: Located on both the 1st and 2nd floors.

The Tombstone Grand Hotel stands as a modern tribute to the enduring spirit of the American West. Situated on a rise overlooking the historic town and the Dragoon Mountains beyond, our location offers the same breathtaking sunsets that cowboys and lawmen witnessed over a century ago.

As a proud member of the Baymont by Wyndham family, we blend the warmth of hometown hospitality with the reliability of a global brand.
